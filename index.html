3 debatable questions

  1. Should tourists pay higher prices than locals at national landmarks?
  2. Is it ethical to visit places that suffer from overtourism?
  3. Are group tours a smarter way to see the world than going solo?

3 Part 2 questions

  1. Describe a hotel or guesthouse where you once stayed that left a strong impression.
  2. Describe a journey by train, bus or boat that you remember well.
  3. Describe a place you would love to visit one day and explain why.

5 Part 3 questions

  1. How has budget airline travel changed people’s holidays?
  2. What responsibilities do travelers have toward the places they visit?
  3. Will virtual reality ever replace real sightseeing?
  4. Why do some people prefer returning to the same destination every year?
  5. How might travel look in fifty years?
